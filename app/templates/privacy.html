{{ icon('lock') }} How your data is handled

  • Data remains on the local machine. The application listens only on 127.0.0.1 and is not reachable from other computers by default.
  • No prompt data is sent to external services. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no AI API and no external fonts or scripts. The scoring, diagnostic and improver tools are deterministic rules that run on your computer.
  • Data is stored in a local SQLite database at {{ db_path }}. Application logs never contain full prompts, PINs or session values by default.
  • You are responsible for protecting local database backups. A backup is a complete copy of your saved prompts, favorites and settings. Store it in a location only you can access.
  • Future external AI integrations may have separate privacy implications. If an AI provider is connected in a later version, prompts sent to it would be handled under that provider’s terms, and this page would be updated.

Responsible AI checklist

  • Remove or anonymise personal and confidential data first.
  • Ask the AI to state assumptions and what should be verified, then verify it.
  • Keep a person responsible for decisions and for anything you send or publish.
  • Follow your organisation’s policy for approved tools.

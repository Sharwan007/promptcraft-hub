What can I do here?

  • Prompt Diagnostic – see what a prompt is missing.

  • Before & After – learn from side-by-side examples.
  • Learning Center – 16 short lessons and a glossary.
  • My Prompts – save, edit, version and export your own prompts.

The Prompt Blueprint

  1. Role – who the AI should act as.
  2. Context – the situation and purpose.
  3. Task – one clear action and deliverable.
  4. Input – the facts or data to use.
  5. Output format – how the answer is structured.
  6. Constraints – limits on length, tone, scope.
  7. Quality criteria – how to judge a good answer.

About the scores

Scores are calculated by transparent, rule-based checks (task clarity, context, role, input, output format, constraints, audience, quality criteria and specificity). They are guidance, not a guarantee, and are not generated by artificial intelligence.
